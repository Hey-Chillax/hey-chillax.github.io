About Me关于我About Me

I am an M.S. student at Nanjing University (Class of 2028), advised by Prof. Haibo Zhou (IEEE Fellow). My research is on large language models, with a focus on multimodal LLMs (video understanding), post-training and efficient inference.

我是南京大学的硕士生（2028 届），导师为周海波教授（IEEE Fellow）。研究方向为大模型，重点关注多模态大模型（视频理解） & 后训练 & 高效推理。

I am good at taking a training pipeline end to end, from data construction to evaluation, and have independently developed and open-sourced two post-training projects: Scaffold-OPD (SFT → OPD → RL) teaches a 1.7B student model long chain-of-thought math from a 4B teacher, and StepCredit-RL (SFT → agentic RL) trains long-horizon, multi-turn tool-calling agents, using step-level credit assignment in the RL stage. I have substantial hands-on training experience: 16,000+ GPU-hours (8×H100) on Slurm clusters, and I have also run extensive experiments on A100 and RTX PRO 6000 GPUs over SSH.

我擅长把训练流程从数据构建到效果评测完整打通，独立完成并开源了两个后训练项目：Scaffold-OPD（SFT → OPD → RL）让 1.7B 学生模型向 4B 教师模型学习长链数学推理；StepCredit-RL（SFT → Agentic RL）训练长程多轮工具调用 Agent，RL 阶段采用步级信用分配。我有丰富的模型训练经验：在 Slurm 集群上累计使用了 16,000+ GPU 小时（8×H100），此外还通过 SSH 在 A100 与 RTX PRO 6000 上开展过大量实验。

I received my B.Eng. from Nanjing University of Posts and Telecommunications, ranked 1st of 541 in my major, with a National Scholarship and four SCI and one EI papers as first author. At Nanjing University I won a national first prize in the “Challenge Cup” competition (2nd of 6 team members). POST-TRAINING后训练2026.09 – 2026.11

Scaffold-OPD

An SFT → OPD → RL post-training pipelineSFT → OPD → RL 后训练全流程

Teaches a 1.7B student long chain-of-thought math from a 4B teacher. The core stage uses Scaffold-OPD: the student generates on its own, the teacher steps in briefly only where the student would miss a reflection or the conclusion, and the whole trajectory is then distilled token by token. The student–teacher relay runs inside vLLM speculative decoding.让 1.7B 学生向 4B 老师学长链数学推理。核心阶段采用 Scaffold-OPD：学生自行生成，老师只在学生想不到的反思或收尾处短暂接管，再对整条轨迹做 token 级蒸馏；师生接力生成基于 vLLM 投机解码实现。

AGENTIC RL2025.09 – 2025.12

StepCredit-RL

SFT → agentic RL for long-horizon, multi-turn tool-calling agents长程多轮工具调用 Agent 的 SFT → Agentic RL 全流程

The RL stage uses StepCredit-RL: steps that start from the same environment state in different trajectories form a group, each step’s advantage is computed from the return that followed it, and this is added to the episode-level advantage — no critic and no extra rollouts.RL 阶段采用 StepCredit-RL：把不同轨迹中处于相同环境状态的步骤归为一组，按之后的回报计算步级优势，再与整局优势相加；不需要 critic，也不需要额外 rollout。
