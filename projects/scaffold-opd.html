Motivation动机Motivation

There are three common ways to teach a 1.7B model long chain-of-thought math from a 4B teacher, and each leaves a gap. SFT imitates the teacher's offline solutions — text the student would not have written itself. RL with an outcome reward is sparse: a solution thousands of tokens long earns a single right-or-wrong signal. On-policy distillation (OPD) has the teacher score every token of the student's own rollout, but it can only grade what the student already writes; a reflection the student never thinks of, such as “Wait, let me check…”, is never demonstrated.

让 1.7B 模型向 4B 老师学习长链数学推理，常见的三种做法各有缺口。SFT 模仿老师离线写好的解答，学到的不是学生自己会写的内容；结果奖励的 RL 太稀疏，几千 token 的解答只换来一个对错信号；在线蒸馏（OPD）让老师给学生自己 rollout 的每个 token 打分，却只能评价学生已经写出的内容，学生想不到的反思（如 “Wait, let me check…”）始终得不到示范。

The core stage of this pipeline uses Scaffold-OPD. While the student generates on its own, the teacher steps in only where the student misses a reflection or the conclusion, writes a short passage, and hands generation back; the whole relayed trajectory is then distilled token by token. Around it I built the full SFT → OPD → RL pipeline and compared the methods on the same student with the same evaluation.

本项目的核心阶段采用 Scaffold-OPD：学生自行生成，老师只在学生漏掉反思或收尾的地方介入，写一小段后交还给学生，再对整条接力轨迹做 token 级蒸馏。围绕这一阶段，我搭建了 SFT → OPD → RL 全流程，并在同一个学生、同一套评测上对比了各种方法。

Pipeline流程Pipeline

  1. Teacher solutions. The teacher writes one solution for each of the 14,116 DAPO-Math problems (up to 16k tokens).
  2. Rejection sampling. A rule-based checker keeps the solutions that finish with a correct answer: 11,158 of them (79%, 36.7M tokens). Rejected solutions are 2.4× longer on average, so filtering keeps long, wrong reasoning out of SFT.
  3. SFT for one epoch on the kept solutions.
  4. Scaffold-OPD, described below.
  5. GRPO on failures. The 2,212 problems that the teacher solved but the student never got right during the Scaffold-OPD stage are trained with an outcome reward.
  1. 老师解答。老师为 DAPO-Math 的 14,116 道题各写一份解答（最长 16k token）。
  2. 拒绝采样。规则判分只保留写完且答对的解答，共 11,158 条（79%，3,669 万 token）。被丢弃的解答平均长 2.4 倍，过滤掉它们，SFT 就不会学进冗长的错误推理。
  3. SFT：在保留的解答上训练 1 轮。
  4. Scaffold-OPD，见下文。
  5. 错题回流 GRPO。老师做对、学生在 Scaffold-OPD 阶段始终没做对的 2,212 道题，用结果奖励训练。

How Scaffold-OPD takes overScaffold-OPD 如何接管

  • Rollout inside speculative decoding, with the roles swapped. The student drafts tokens and the teacher verifies them in vLLM. While verifying, the teacher already computes its own next-token distribution, so finding a takeover point costs no extra forward pass. Takeover passages are produced by speculative sampling, so their distribution is exactly the teacher's own.
  • When to take over. The teacher's most likely next token is a reflection word (45 of them, such as Wait and But) that is not among the student's top-5. A concluding trigger also fires when the teacher wants to start the final answer (### ✅ Final Answer) while the student keeps going.
  • How much. The teacher writes at most three paragraphs (256 tokens) before handing back. Each answer allows at most two takeovers, and it ends once the second one is written.
  • Training signal. Every token is tagged with its author and the tags are passed back to the trainer, so the loss can tell student and teacher segments apart. A separate teacher scorer computes the teacher's log-probabilities for the whole answer.
  • 在投机解码里 rollout，师生角色对调。学生在 vLLM 里写草稿，老师负责验证。老师验证时已经算出自己的下一个 token 分布，所以判断是否接管不需要额外的前向计算。接管段经投机采样生成，分布与老师直接续写严格一致。
  • 何时接管。老师最想写的下一个 token 是反思词（共 45 个，如 Wait、But），且不在学生的 top-5 里。另有收尾触发：老师想开始写最终答案（### ✅ Final Answer）而学生还在往下写时，也会接管。
  • 接管多少。老师最多写 3 段（256 token）就交还。每条回答最多接管 2 次，第 2 次接管写完，回答就结束。
  • 训练信号。每个 token 都标记由谁写，标记回传训练端，损失据此区分师生段。另有一个老师打分器，计算整条回答的老师 log-prob。

In the code, this stage is METHOD=scaffold in pipeline/train_stage.sh; CONCLUDE_TRIGGER=1 turns on the concluding trigger.代码里这一阶段是 pipeline/train_stage.sh 的 METHOD=scaffold，CONCLUDE_TRIGGER=1 打开收尾触发。

Analysis分析Analysis

Where the gain comes from提分从哪里来

The advantage over OPD depends on the evaluation budget: the two are tied at 8k tokens, and Scaffold-OPD leads by 2.8 points at 16k and by 5.6 at 32k. OPD stops improving at 16k because it already finishes 93% of its AIME answers within that budget. Students trained with Scaffold-OPD reflect about twice as often (2.98 vs 1.50 “Wait” per 1k tokens; base model 0.21) and write longer AIME answers (14.5k vs 8.6k tokens on average). Even when Scaffold-OPD is capped at 16k and OPD keeps the full 32k, Scaffold-OPD still leads by 2.7 ± 0.9, so roughly half of the gain is better reasoning and half is longer reasoning. The extra length goes mostly to problems the student does not solve: correct AIME answers average 7.3k tokens, wrong ones 16.8k.

相对 OPD 的优势取决于评测预算：8k token 时两者打平，16k 时 Scaffold-OPD 领先 2.8 分，32k 时领先 5.6 分。OPD 到 16k 就不再提升，因为它在 16k 内已经写完 93% 的 AIME 回答。Scaffold-OPD 训出的学生反思次数约为 OPD 的 2 倍（每千 token 的 “Wait” 2.98 对 1.50，基座 0.21），AIME 回答也更长（平均 14.5k 对 8.6k token）。即使把 Scaffold-OPD 限制在 16k、OPD 用满 32k，Scaffold-OPD 仍领先 2.7 ± 0.9 分，所以提分大约一半来自推理更好，一半来自推理更长。多写的部分主要花在没解出的题上：答对的 AIME 回答平均 7.3k token，答错的 16.8k。

Why GRPO lagsGRPO 为什么落后

With only an outcome reward, GRPO stops improving early and its answers stay short (AIME 2.8k–4.4k tokens). A larger batch helps: 128 problems × 8 rollouts per step beats 32 × 8 by 3.8 points and is still rising at step 60. But with 2.6× the GPU-hours and 12× the rollouts it gains 5.6 points, against 21 for Scaffold-OPD. For a small model, long reasoning is learned mainly from the teacher's dense per-token signal.

只有结果奖励时，GRPO 很早就停止提升，回答始终很短（AIME 2.8k–4.4k token）。加大 batch 有帮助：每步 128 题 × 8 条比 32 题 × 8 条高 3.8 分，第 60 步仍在上升。但它用了 2.6 倍卡时和 12 倍 rollout，只提高 5.6 分，而 Scaffold-OPD 提高 21 分。对小模型来说，长推理主要靠老师稠密的逐 token 信号来学。

The concluding trigger: the gain is in solving, not finishing收尾触发：提分来自解题，而不是收尾

Besides reflection points, the teacher can also take over when it wants to start the final answer while the student keeps going. To find the right marker I counted the endings of 6,880 answers: the conclusion is signalled by a header such as ### ✅ Final Answer, not by words like “Therefore”. Because ✅ is split into two tokens, the rule also looks at the previous token (###), carried across speculative-decoding steps for each request. Turning this trigger off barely changes the result — 46.4 vs 46.9 at step 40 (a difference of 0.46 ± 0.95) and 43.8 vs 45.2 at step 60 — so the improvement over OPD comes from solving more problems, not from finishing answers. Two observations explain it. Lost points come from problems the student cannot solve, not from answers it cannot finish. And because an answer ends after its second takeover, 56–77% of training answers used up both takeovers on reflections and were cut off before the conclusion, so the concluding trigger accounts for only 0.9% of teacher segments.

除了反思处，老师想开始写最终答案而学生还在往下写时，也可以接管。为了找准标记，我统计了 6,880 条回答的结尾：收尾由 ### ✅ Final Answer 这类标题标志，而不是 “Therefore” 这类连接词。✅ 会被分词拆成两个 token，所以规则还要看前一个 token（###），并按请求跨投机解码步记录。关闭收尾触发后结果几乎不变：第 40 步 46.4 对 46.9（差 0.46 ± 0.95），第 60 步 43.8 对 45.2，说明相对 OPD 的提分来自解出更多题，而不是更会收尾。原因有二：失分主要是题解不出来，而不是写不完；每条回答第 2 次接管写完就结束，56–77% 的训练回答用完 2 次反思接管就被截断，写不到收尾，所以收尾触发只占老师段的 0.9%。

The full pipeline is not worth it at this scale这个规模下全流程不划算

Each stage helps — SFT +12.6, Scaffold-OPD +4.6, GRPO on failures +4.0 — but the final 46.6 only ties Scaffold-OPD alone (+0.14 ± 1.05), at about 4× the GPU-hours (52.4 vs 13.2). SFT warm-up even hurts: Scaffold-OPD trained after SFT scores 3.9 ± 1.0 points below the same number of steps from the base model, with shorter answers (AIME 10.1k vs 14.5k tokens). The pipeline’s final answers are also shorter (11.9k), which is why it comes out ahead within the 16k training budget (+2.3 ± 0.9). At this scale, distilling directly from the base model is the better trade.

每一段都有提升：SFT +12.6，Scaffold-OPD +4.6，错题回流 GRPO +4.0。但最终的 46.6 只与单独做 Scaffold-OPD 持平（+0.14 ± 1.05），卡时却是后者的约 4 倍（52.4 对 13.2）。先做 SFT 甚至有害：SFT 之后再训练的 Scaffold-OPD，比从基座训练同样步数低 3.9 ± 1.0 分，回答也更短（AIME 10.1k 对 14.5k token）。全流程最终的回答同样更短（11.9k），所以在训练用的 16k 预算下它反而更高（+2.3 ± 0.9）。在这个规模下，从基座直接蒸馏更划算。

How far to trust these numbers这些数字可信到什么程度

  • Every result is a single seed. The runs with and without the concluding trigger share the same data order and differ in under 1% of teacher segments, so together they act as a near-rerun: they differ by 0.5–1.4 points, which underestimates seed-to-seed variance.
  • AIME has only 30 problems per year; even with 16 samples per problem, the per-benchmark standard error is large, so the 4-benchmark average and paired differences are what this page reports.
  • One Scaffold-OPD run hit a rollout-side generation anomaly at step 19 (looping outputs that disappeared at the next step, once the weights were re-synced); it is left out of the curves.
  • Contamination check (8-gram): no overlap for AIME 2024 and 2025; one AIME 2026 problem and 12 MATH-500 problems (2.4%) overlap with the training set.
  • 所有结果都是单种子。开和关收尾触发的两次训练数据顺序相同，只在不到 1% 的老师段上有区别，可以看作近似重跑：两者相差 0.5–1.4 分，这低估了换种子时的波动。
  • AIME 每年只有 30 题，即使每题采样 16 次，单项的标准误也不小，所以本页主要报告 4 项平均和逐题配对差。
  • 一次 Scaffold-OPD 训练在第 19 步出现生成端异常（输出循环，下一步重新同步权重后消失），曲线中已剔除。
  • 8-gram 污染检查：AIME 2024、2025 无重复；AIME 2026 有 1 题、MATH-500 有 12 题（2.4%）与训练集重复。
