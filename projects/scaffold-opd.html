Motivation动机

A 1.7B model can learn long chain-of-thought math from a 4B teacher in three common ways, and each leaves a gap. SFT imitates offline teacher solutions — text the student would not have written itself. RL with an outcome reward is sparse: one right-or-wrong bit for a solution thousands of tokens long. On-policy distillation (OPD) lets the teacher score every token of the student's own rollout, but it can only grade what the student already writes; a reflection the student never thinks of (“Wait, let me check…”) never gets demonstrated.

让 1.7B 模型向 4B 老师学长链数学推理，常见的三种做法各有短板。SFT 模仿的是老师离线写好的解答，不是学生自己会写的内容。结果奖励的 RL 很稀疏：几千 token 的解答只有一个对错信号。在线蒸馏（OPD）让老师给学生自己 rollout 的每个 token 打分，但只能评价学生已经写出的内容；学生想不到的反思（“Wait, let me check…”）始终得不到示范。

The core stage of this pipeline adopts Takeover OPD: during the student's own rollout, the teacher steps in only where the student misses a reflection or the conclusion, writes a short passage, and hands generation back. The whole relayed trajectory is then distilled token by token. Around it, I built and compared the full SFT → OPD → RL pipeline on the same student and the same evaluation.

本项目的核心阶段采用 Takeover OPD：学生自己 rollout，老师只在学生漏掉反思或收尾的地方介入，写一小段后把生成交还给学生，再对整条接力轨迹做 token 级蒸馏。围绕它，我搭建了 SFT → OPD → RL 全流程，并在同一个学生、同一套评测上做了对比。

Pipeline流程

  1. Teacher data. The teacher writes one solution per training problem (14,116 DAPO-Math problems, ≤16k tokens).
  2. Rejection sampling. A rule-based answer checker keeps solutions that finish and are correct: 11,158 of them (79%, 36.7M tokens). Discarded solutions are 2.4× longer on average, so filtering keeps long wrong reasoning out of SFT.
  3. SFT for one epoch on the kept solutions.
  4. Takeover OPD (below).
  5. GRPO on failures. Problems the teacher solved but the student never got right during OPD (2,212) are trained with an outcome reward.
  1. 老师轨迹生成。老师为每道训练题写一份解答（DAPO-Math 14,116 题，≤16k token）。
  2. 拒绝采样。规则判分保留写完且答对的解答，共 11,158 条（79%，3,669 万 token）。被丢弃的解答平均长 2.4 倍，过滤掉它们，SFT 就不会学进冗长的错误推理。
  3. SFT：在保留的解答上训练 1 轮。
  4. Takeover OPD（见下）。
  5. 错题回流 GRPO。老师做对、学生在 OPD 阶段始终没拿到分的题（2,212 道）用结果奖励训练。

How a takeover works接管是怎么发生的

  • Rollout inside speculative decoding, roles swapped. The student drafts tokens and the teacher verifies them in vLLM. While verifying, the teacher already has its own next-token distribution, so finding a takeover point costs no extra forward pass, and the teacher's passages are themselves accelerated by speculative decoding.
  • When to take over. The teacher's most likely next token is a reflection word (45 of them, such as Wait and But) that is not in the student's top-5. An optional concluding trigger also fires when the teacher wants to start the final answer (### ✅ Final Answer) while the student keeps going.
  • How much. The teacher writes up to 3 paragraphs (≤256 tokens) and hands back; each answer allows at most 2 takeovers.
  • Training signal. Every token is tagged with its author; the tags are passed back to the trainer over IPC, so the loss can treat student and teacher segments separately. A separate teacher scorer computes the teacher's log-probabilities for the whole answer.
  • 在投机解码里 rollout，角色对调。学生在 vLLM 里写草稿，老师验证。老师验证时已经算出了自己的下一个 token 分布，所以找接管点不需要额外的前向计算，老师写的段落本身也由投机解码加速。
  • 何时接管。老师最想写的下一个 token 是反思词（共 45 个，如 Wait、But），且不在学生的 top-5 里。可选的收尾触发：老师想开始写最终答案（### ✅ Final Answer）而学生还在往下写时，也会接管。
  • 接管多少。老师最多写 3 段（≤256 token）后交还；每条回答最多接管 2 次。
  • 训练信号。每个 token 都标记由谁写，标记经 IPC 传回训练端，损失据此区分师生段。另有一个老师打分器，计算整条回答的老师 log-prob。

In the code, this stage is METHOD=scaffold in pipeline/train_stage.sh; CONCLUDE_TRIGGER=1 turns on the concluding trigger.代码里这一阶段是 pipeline/train_stage.sh 的 METHOD=scaffold，CONCLUDE_TRIGGER=1 打开收尾触发。

Analysis分析

Where the gain comes from提分从哪里来

The advantage over OPD depends on the evaluation budget: the two are tied at 8k tokens, then Takeover OPD leads by 2.8 points at 16k and 5.6 at 32k. OPD stops improving at 16k because it has already finished 93% of its AIME answers there. Takeover-trained students reflect about twice as often (2.98 vs 1.50 “Wait” per 1k tokens; the base model 0.21) and write longer AIME answers (14.5k vs 8.6k tokens on average). Even when Takeover OPD is capped at 16k and OPD keeps the full 32k, Takeover OPD still leads by 2.7 ± 0.9 — so roughly half of the gain is better reasoning and half is longer reasoning. The extra length goes mostly into problems it does not solve: correct AIME answers average 7.3k tokens, wrong ones 16.8k.

相对 OPD 的优势取决于评测预算：8k 时两者打平，16k 时 Takeover OPD 高 2.8，32k 时高 5.6。OPD 到 16k 就不再涨，因为它在 16k 内已经写完了 93% 的 AIME 回答。Takeover OPD 训出的学生反思次数约是 OPD 的 2 倍（每千 token 的 “Wait” 2.98 对 1.50，基座 0.21），AIME 回答也更长（平均 14.5k 对 8.6k token）。即使把 Takeover OPD 限在 16k、OPD 用满 32k，Takeover OPD 仍高 2.7 ± 0.9，所以提分大约一半来自推理更好，一半来自推理更长。多写的部分主要花在没解出的题上：答对的 AIME 回答平均 7.3k token，答错的 16.8k。

Why GRPO lagsGRPO 为什么落后

With only an outcome reward, GRPO stops improving early and its answers stay short (AIME 2.8k–4.4k tokens). A larger batch helps — 128 problems × 8 rollouts per step beats 32 × 8 by 3.8 points and is still rising at step 60 — but at 2.6× the GPU hours and 12× the rollouts it gains 5.6 points, against 21 for Takeover OPD. For a small model, long reasoning is learned mainly from the teacher's dense per-token signal.

只有结果奖励时，GRPO 很早就停涨，回答始终很短（AIME 2.8k–4.4k token）。加大 batch 有用：每步 128 题 × 8 条比 32 题 × 8 条高 3.8 分，第 60 步还在涨。但它用了 2.6 倍卡时、12 倍 rollout，只提高 5.6 分，Takeover OPD 提高 21 分。小模型学长推理，主要靠老师稠密的逐 token 信号。

What did not work: the concluding trigger没有效果的尝试：收尾触发

I added a second trigger so the teacher could also take over to write the final answer, aiming to help the student finish within a tight budget. To find the right marker I counted endings in 6,880 answers: the conclusion is signalled by a header such as ### ✅ Final Answer, not by words like “Therefore”. Because ✅ is split into two tokens, the rule also looks at the previous token (###), carried across speculative-decoding steps for each request. An inference-only probe raised the AIME answer rate by 4.4 points, short of the 10-point bar set in advance; after training, the two runs are within noise (+0.46 ± 0.95 at step 40, +1.39 ± 0.94 at step 60). Two reasons: lost points come from problems the student cannot solve rather than answers it cannot finish, and with at most two takeovers per answer, 56–77% of training answers end before the conclusion, so the trigger fires in only 0.9% of teacher segments. Making it matter would need a different truncation rule — effectively a different method.

我加了第二种触发，让老师也能接管来写最终答案，希望学生在紧预算下更早写完。为了找准标记，我统计了 6,880 条回答的结尾：收尾由 ### ✅ Final Answer 这类标题标志，而不是 “Therefore” 这类连接词。✅ 会被分词拆成两段，所以规则还要看前一个 token（###），并按请求跨投机解码步记录。只推理不训练的预研中，AIME 作答率只提高 4.4 个点，没达到事先定的 10 个点门槛；训练后两次运行的差距在噪声以内（第 40 步 +0.46 ± 0.95，第 60 步 +1.39 ± 0.94）。原因有二：失分主要是题解不出来，而不是写不完；每条回答最多接管 2 次，56–77% 的训练回答在收尾前就结束了，收尾触发只占老师段的 0.9%。要让它起作用，需要改截断规则，那已经是另一个方法。

The full pipeline is not worth it at this scale这个规模下全流程不划算

Each stage helps — SFT +12.6, Takeover OPD +4.6, GRPO on failures +4.0 — but the final 46.6 ties Takeover OPD alone (+0.14 ± 1.05) at about 4× the GPU hours (52.4 vs 13.2). SFT warm-up even lowers the following Takeover OPD by 3.9 ± 1.0 at the same step count, and its answers are shorter (AIME 10.1k vs 14.5k tokens). That shorter length is also why the pipeline wins within the 16k training budget (+2.3 ± 0.9). At this scale, distilling directly from the base model is the better trade.

每一段都有效：SFT +12.6，Takeover OPD +4.6，错题回流 GRPO +4.0。但最终的 46.6 和单独做 Takeover OPD 持平（+0.14 ± 1.05），卡时却多约 4 倍（52.4 对 13.2）。先做 SFT 反而让后面同步数的 Takeover OPD 低 3.9 ± 1.0，回答也更短（AIME 10.1k 对 14.5k token）；也正因为更短，全流程在训练用的 16k 预算下更高（+2.3 ± 0.9）。在这个规模下，从基座直接蒸馏更划算。

How far to trust these numbers这些数字可信到什么程度

  • Every result is a single seed. The concluding-trigger run is a near-rerun of the main run (same data order; the extra trigger fires in under 1% of teacher segments): the two differ by 0.5–1.4 points, which underestimates seed-to-seed variance.
  • AIME has only 30 problems per year; with 16 samples per problem the per-benchmark standard error is still large, so the 4-benchmark average and paired differences are reported.
  • One Takeover OPD run hit a rollout-side generation anomaly at step 19 (looping outputs, recovered the next step after weights were re-synced); it is excluded from the curves.
  • Contamination check (8-gram): no overlap for AIME 2024 and 2025; 1 AIME 2026 problem and 12 MATH-500 problems (2.4%) overlap with the training set.
  • 所有结果都是单种子。收尾触发那次训练和主实验数据顺序相同、多出的触发不到老师段的 1%，可以看作近似重跑：两者差 0.5–1.4 分。这低估了换种子时的波动。
  • AIME 每年只有 30 题，每题采 16 次后单项的标准误仍然不小，所以主要报 4 项平均和逐题配对差。
  • 一次 Takeover OPD 训练在第 19 步出现生成端异常（输出循环，下一步重新同步权重后自动恢复），曲线中已剔除。
  • 8-gram 污染检查：AIME 2024、2025 无重复；AIME 2026 有 1 题、MATH-500 有 12 题（2.4%）与训练集重复。

End-to-end pipeline全流程流水线

Teacher data → rule-graded rejection sampling (multi-process) → SFT → Takeover OPD → failure mining from rollout records → GRPO, with evaluation between stages and stage-level resume.

老师轨迹生成 → 规则判分拒绝采样（多进程）→ SFT → Takeover OPD → 从 rollout 记录里挖错题 → GRPO；阶段之间自动评测，按阶段断点续跑。

Takeover training metrics接管训练的监控指标

Teacher-token share, number and length of takeover segments, position of the first takeover, k1 and entropy for student and teacher segments, scores of answers that used up the takeover budget; CPU tests.

老师 token 占比、接管段数和段长、第一次接管的位置、师生段各自的 k1 与熵、用满接管额度的回答得分等；附 CPU 单测。

Evaluation aligned with public baselines, accuracy recomputed under token budgets, per-problem paired standard errors, 8-gram contamination check, statistics of concluding markers.

Training stability and efficiency训练稳定性与效率

W&B offline runs now finish properly on exit (they used to lose the last steps), with a CPU test; a watchdog for evaluations that hang after writing results. I also traced the training step's memory and time to the full-vocabulary logits and switched on the framework's existing chunked log-prob computation: about 27% faster per 1k tokens, peak reserved memory 75 → 39 GB, identical numerics.

W&B 离线运行在退出时正确结束（之前会丢最后几步），附 CPU 单测；评测写完结果后卡死的看门狗。另外定位到训练段的显存和耗时瓶颈在整张词表的 logits，启用框架已有的分块 log-prob 计算：每千 token 训练快约 27%，显存峰值 75 → 39 GB，数值不变。
