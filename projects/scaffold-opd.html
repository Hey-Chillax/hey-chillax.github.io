Motivation动机

Teaching a 1.7B student long chain-of-thought math from a 4B teacher, the usual approaches each fall short. SFT only imitates solutions the teacher wrote offline. RL with an outcome reward is sparse: thousands of tokens earn a single right-or-wrong signal. On-policy distillation (OPD) scores every token of the student's own rollout, but it cannot teach what the student never thinks of — a reflection such as “Wait, let me check…”.

让 1.7B 学生向 4B 老师学长链数学推理，常见做法各有短板：SFT 只模仿老师离线写好的解答；结果奖励的 RL 太稀疏，几千 token 只换来一个对错信号；在线蒸馏（OPD）能给学生自己写的每个 token 打分，却教不会学生想不到的东西，比如 “Wait, let me check…” 这样的反思。

This project uses Scaffold-OPD: the student generates on its own, and the teacher steps in briefly only where the student misses a reflection or the conclusion, writes a short passage and hands back; the whole relayed trajectory is then distilled token by token. Around it I built the full SFT → OPD → RL pipeline and compared the approaches on the same student with the same evaluation.

本项目采用 Scaffold-OPD：学生自行生成，老师只在学生漏掉反思或收尾时短暂接管，写一小段后交还，再对整条接力轨迹做 token 级蒸馏。围绕它，我搭建了 SFT → OPD → RL 全流程，在同一学生、同一评测下对比了各种做法。

Method方法

  • Relay generation. Inside vLLM speculative decoding, the student drafts and the teacher verifies. While verifying, the teacher has already computed its own next-token distribution, so deciding whether to take over costs no extra forward pass; takeover passages are produced by speculative sampling, so their distribution is exactly the teacher's own.
  • When to take over. The teacher's most likely next token is a reflection word (45 of them, such as Wait and But) that is not in the student's top-5. A concluding trigger also fires when the teacher wants to start the final answer (### ✅ Final Answer) while the student keeps going; I implemented this part in the verification step.
  • How much. Each takeover writes at most three paragraphs (256 tokens) before handing back; an answer allows at most two takeovers and ends once the second is written.
  • Training signal. Author tags travel with the tokens back to the trainer; a teacher scorer on a separate GPU computes the teacher's log-probabilities for the whole answer.
  • 接力生成。在 vLLM 投机解码中，学生写草稿、老师做验证。老师验证时已算出自己的下一 token 分布，判断是否接管不需要额外的前向计算；接管段经投机采样生成，分布与老师直接续写严格一致。
  • 何时接管。老师最想写的下一个 token 是反思词（共 45 个，如 Wait、But），且不在学生的 top-5 里。另有收尾触发：老师想开始写最终答案（### ✅ Final Answer）而学生还在往下写；这部分由我在验证步中实现。
  • 接管多少。每次最多写 3 段（256 token）就交还；每条回答最多接管 2 次，第 2 次写完回答即结束。
  • 训练信号。作者标记随 token 回传训练端；单独一张卡上的老师打分器算出整条回答的老师 log-prob。

In the code, this stage is METHOD=scaffold in pipeline/train_stage.sh; CONCLUDE_TRIGGER=1 turns on the concluding trigger.代码里这一阶段是 pipeline/train_stage.sh 的 METHOD=scaffold，CONCLUDE_TRIGGER=1 打开收尾触发。

The full pipeline全流程

  1. Teacher solutions. The teacher writes one solution for each of the 14,116 DAPO-Math problems (up to 16k tokens).
  2. Rejection sampling. A rule-based checker keeps the 11,158 solutions (79%) that finish with a correct answer; rejected ones are 2.4× longer on average, so filtering keeps long, wrong reasoning out of SFT.
  3. SFT for one epoch on the kept solutions.
  4. Scaffold-OPD, described above.
  5. GRPO on failures. The 2,212 problems the teacher solved but the student never got right during the Scaffold-OPD stage are trained with an outcome reward.
  1. 老师解答。老师为 DAPO-Math 的 14,116 道题各写一份解答（最长 16k token）。
  2. 拒绝采样。规则判分只保留写完且答对的 11,158 条（79%）；被丢弃的解答平均长 2.4 倍，过滤掉它们，SFT 就不会学进冗长的错误推理。
  3. SFT：在保留的解答上训练 1 轮。
  4. Scaffold-OPD：见上文。
  5. 错题回流 GRPO。老师做对、学生在 Scaffold-OPD 阶段始终没做对的 2,212 道题，用结果奖励训练。

Analysis分析

Where the gain comes from提分从哪里来

The two are tied at an 8k budget; Scaffold-OPD leads by 2.8 points at 16k and by 5.6 at 32k. OPD has finished 93% of its AIME answers within 16k and stops improving there; students trained with Scaffold-OPD reflect more (2.98 vs 1.50 “Wait” per 1k tokens; base model 0.21) and write longer AIME answers (14.5k vs 8.6k tokens on average). Even capped at 16k against OPD's full 32k, Scaffold-OPD still leads by 2.7 ± 0.9: roughly half of the gain is better reasoning and half is longer reasoning. The extra length goes mostly to problems the student does not solve — correct AIME answers average 7.3k tokens, wrong ones 16.8k.

8k 预算时两者持平，16k 时 Scaffold-OPD 领先 2.8 分，32k 时领先 5.6 分。OPD 在 16k 内已写完 93% 的 AIME 回答，之后不再提升；Scaffold-OPD 训出的学生反思更多（每千 token 的 “Wait” 2.98 对 1.50，基座 0.21），AIME 回答也更长（平均 14.5k 对 8.6k token）。把 Scaffold-OPD 限制在 16k、OPD 用满 32k，Scaffold-OPD 仍领先 2.7 ± 0.9：提分大约一半来自推理更好，一半来自推理更长。多写的部分主要花在没解出的题上：答对的 AIME 回答平均 7.3k token，答错的 16.8k。

Why GRPO lagsGRPO 为什么落后

With only an outcome reward, GRPO stops improving early and its answers stay short (AIME 2.8k–4.4k tokens). A larger batch helps — 128 problems × 8 rollouts per step beats 32 × 8 by 3.8 points and is still rising at step 60 — but 2.6× the GPU-hours and 12× the rollouts buy only +5.6, against +21 for Scaffold-OPD. A small model learns long reasoning mainly from the teacher's dense per-token signal.

只有结果奖励时，GRPO 很早就停止提升，回答始终很短（AIME 2.8k–4.4k token）。加大 batch 有用：每步 128 题 × 8 条比 32 题 × 8 条高 3.8 分，第 60 步仍在上升；但 2.6 倍卡时、12 倍 rollout 只换来 +5.6，Scaffold-OPD 是 +21。小模型学长推理，主要靠老师逐 token 的稠密信号。

The concluding trigger: the gain is in solving, not finishing收尾触发：提分来自解题，而不是收尾

To find the right marker I counted the endings of 6,880 answers: the conclusion is signalled by a header such as ### ✅ Final Answer, not by words like “Therefore”. Because ✅ is split into two tokens, the rule also looks at the previous token (###), tracked per request across speculative-decoding steps. Turning the trigger off barely changes the result (46.4 vs 46.9 at step 40; 43.8 vs 45.2 at step 60). Two reasons: lost points come from problems the student cannot solve, not answers it cannot finish; and because an answer ends after its second takeover, 56–77% of training answers use up both takeovers on reflections and are cut off before the conclusion, so the concluding trigger accounts for only 0.9% of teacher segments.

为了找准收尾标记，我统计了 6,880 条回答的结尾：收尾由 ### ✅ Final Answer 这类标题标志，而不是 “Therefore” 这类连接词。✅ 会被分词拆成两个 token，所以规则还要看前一个 token（###），并按请求跨投机解码步记录。关闭收尾触发后结果几乎不变（第 40 步 46.4 对 46.9，第 60 步 43.8 对 45.2）。原因有二：失分主要是题解不出来，而不是写不完；每条回答第 2 次接管后就结束，56–77% 的训练回答用完 2 次反思接管就被截断，写不到收尾，收尾触发只占老师段的 0.9%。

The full pipeline is not worth it at this scale这个规模下全流程不划算

Each stage helps — SFT +12.6, Scaffold-OPD +4.6, GRPO on failures +4.0 — but the final 46.6 only ties Scaffold-OPD alone (+0.14 ± 1.05) at about 4× the GPU-hours (52.4 vs 13.2). SFT warm-up even hurts: Scaffold-OPD trained after SFT scores 3.9 ± 1.0 below the same number of steps from the base model, with shorter answers (AIME 10.1k vs 14.5k tokens). At this scale, distilling directly from the base model is the better trade.

每一段都有提升：SFT +12.6，Scaffold-OPD +4.6，错题回流 GRPO +4.0。但最终的 46.6 只与直接做 Scaffold-OPD 持平（+0.14 ± 1.05），卡时却是后者的约 4 倍（52.4 对 13.2）。先做 SFT 甚至有害：SFT 之后再训练的 Scaffold-OPD，比从基座训练同样步数低 3.9 ± 1.0 分，回答也更短（AIME 10.1k 对 14.5k token）。这个规模下，从基座直接蒸馏更划算。
